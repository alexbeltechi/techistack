# Genres

Starting points per kind of work. When a genre gets curated for real, give it
its own file (as `portrait.md`) and grow it from the user's lessons.

| Genre | What the viewer looks for | Typical count | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portrait | The person, how the photographer sees people | 5–8 | See `portrait.md` |
| Fashion / lookbook | The garments: fit, fabric, movement; then mood | 8–15 | Full looks first, then details; one frame per look minimum |
| Fine art / painting | The work itself, undistorted; scale | 1–6 | Straight-on, true colour; one detail shot for texture; dimensions in the text |
| Travel | Place and atmosphere, a sense of being there | 6–12 | Wide → people → details; chronological |
| Product / design case study | The problem, the role, what was made, the result | 4–10 visuals in a story | Problem → role → designed & built → result; text between images |
| Architecture / interiors | Space, light, how it's lived in | 6–12 | Exterior → threshold → interior → detail |
| Event / music | Energy, the people | 6–10 | The peak moment first |

Across all genres: fewer, stronger; open on the best frame; say what it is
plainly.
